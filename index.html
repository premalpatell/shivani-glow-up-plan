Instructions

How to read your plan

Every exercise card uses the same language. Here's what each piece means:

01
Exercise number

Your order for the session — go top to bottom, one exercise at a time. Finish all sets of one before moving to the next.

3 × 10–12
Sets × reps

"3 × 10–12" = do 10–12 repetitions, rest, and repeat until you've done 3 rounds. Hit the top number with good form? Add a little weight next week.

rest 90 s
Rest between sets

Breathe for this long between sets. The pink ⏱ Rest Timer button (bottom-right) counts it down and chimes when it's time.

Chin tucked, ribs down…
Coach's cue

The one thing to think about while doing the movement. Read it before your first set.

Form video

Tap any thumbnail to watch the lesson right there on the card. Watch it once before your first-ever attempt of an exercise.

YouTube ↗
Backup link

If a video won't play inside the page (rare), this opens the same video in the YouTube app or a new tab.

Complete
Mark it done

Tap after your last set — it turns green ✓ Completed and your day's progress bar fills. Finish a whole day and you get confetti.

Foundations

The golden rules

📈

Progressive Overload

Finish every set at the top of the rep range with clean form? Add a little weight next time. That is how a body changes.

🍳

Protein Daily

Roughly 1.6–2 g per kg of bodyweight, spread across the day, keeps muscle toning and recovery on track.

🌙

Sleep 7–9 Hours

The sculpting happens at night. Guard your sleep like a training session.

💧

Hydrate

2.5–3 litres a day. Bring a bottle, sip between sets.

🐢

Form Before Weight

Slow, controlled reps beat heavy sloppy ones — every single time.

🔥

Consistency Wins

Missing one day is human. Never miss two. Results live in the months, not the weeks.

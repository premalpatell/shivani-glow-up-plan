Foundations

The golden rules

📈

Progressive Overload

Finish every set at the top of the rep range with clean form? Add a little weight next time. That's how a body changes.

🍳

Protein Daily

Roughly 0.7–1 g per lb of bodyweight, spread across the day, keeps toning and recovery on track.

🌙

Sleep 7–9 Hours

The sculpting happens at night. Guard sleep like a training session.

💧

Hydrate

About a gallon a day. Bring a bottle, sip between sets.

🐢

Form Before Weight

Slow, controlled reps beat heavy sloppy ones — every single time.

🔥

Consistency Wins

Missing one day is human. Never miss two. Results live in months, not weeks.
